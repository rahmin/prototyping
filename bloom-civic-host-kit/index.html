A warm, editorial system for civic work.

Paper and ink rather than white and black, with pine for structure and gold for emphasis. Built for pages that carry a lot of text and data and still need to feel like they were made by people, for people.

The signature band

Use the dark band to mark a turn.

Once or twice on a page — to open, to change register, or to close. Two bands back to back read as a mistake.

01

Paper, not white

Every surface is warm. White appears nowhere in the palette; the lightest tone is paper-2.

02

Two accents, ranked

Pine carries structure and affirmative action. Gold is emphasis, and is rationed.

03

Mono does the labeling

Every eyebrow, tag, count, and unit is DM Mono, uppercase, tracked to .04em.

Components

Cards carry the content.

Depth comes from the paper/paper-2 pairing and a hairline border. There are no drop shadows in this system.
