What Utahns said

About 540 Utahns weighed in before the Forum.

In an online opinion-mapping poll, participants reacted to statements written by other Utahns and added statements of their own. The poll mapped where people already agree across political lines, and those areas of agreement shape what the forty delegates will discuss.

Who is deliberating

Forty Utahns, chosen by lottery.

Invitations went out by mail to randomly selected households across Utah. Everyone who replied went into a pool, and the final panel of forty was drawn from that pool to match the state on six measures.

Nobody applied and nobody was appointed. Delegates are paid for their time, and they are described here as a group: no names and no photographs are published.

The process

Five stages, from a poll to published recommendations.

  1. Complete · Spring 2026

    Three-county opinion poll

    An online poll in three counties surfaced the questions residents were already raising.

  2. Complete · Summer 2026

    Community conversations and the Cache County Forum

    In-person sessions tested what the poll found, and a first forum in Cache County published its responses in full.

  3. Complete · Summer 2026

    Delegate selection

    Mailed invitations, a pool of respondents, and a final draw of forty matched to the state.

  4. Next · September 18–19, 2026

    The Utah Solutions Forum

    Two days in Lehi. Delegates hear from people who disagree with each other, then work toward recommendations.

  5. Ahead · Date to be confirmed

    Recommendations published

    The delegates' conclusions are published in full and delivered to the bodies that can act on them.

What is a civic assembly?

A jury for a policy question.

01

Chosen by lottery

Everyday people, drawn at random rather than by application or appointment, and paid for their time.

02

They hear both sides

Delegates hear from experts and advocates who disagree with each other, then question them directly.

03

Supermajority to adopt

A recommendation is adopted only when a supermajority of the panel supports it.

Nonpartisan notice

The Utah Solutions Forum is nonpartisan. It does not support or oppose any political party, candidate, or ballot measure. Presenters, facilitators, staff, partner organizations, and delegates may not conduct campaign activity in connection with the Forum. Recommendations produced by the Forum are the delegates' own conclusions and are not the positions of any organizing or funding organization. Nothing on this site is a request to contact a public official about legislation.
